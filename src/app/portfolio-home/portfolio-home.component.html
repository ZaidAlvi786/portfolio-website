About

Who Am I?

I am an Operations and Administration Manager with over 17 years of experience supporting UK retail and e-commerce giants. My expertise spans third-party customer services, buying & merchandising support, purchase order management, and end-to-end supply chain delivery. I am recognised for my ability to manage high-volume environments through strong SLA governance and KPI-driven execution.

Throughout my career, I have led cross-functional teams across warehousing, DC operations, and distribution. I have worked directly with international brands like Matalan, Debenhams, Very, and Secret Sales, ensuring smooth coordination between buying, fulfilment, and supply chain functions. My focus is always on process improvement, SOP compliance, and driving business impact through operational excellence.
